Case Study: Moletsi Traditional Council

How we built a digital ecosystem that connected a community.

The Challenge

The Moletsi Traditional Council had no central digital presence. Information was shared through word of mouth, printed notices, and phone calls – leading to confusion, missed events, and administrative overload.

The Solution

We built a custom ASP.NET website with:

  • Admin dashboard – staff could publish news, events, and documents without needing technical skills
  • Document library – council minutes, bylaws, and public notices available online
  • Event calendar – community members could see upcoming events and RSVP
  • Social media integration – Facebook and Twitter feeds displayed on the homepage
