Case Study: Government School Digital Transformation

A complete digital solution for 1,400+ learners, teachers, and parents – fully compliant with South African education regulations.

The Challenge

A government school in Cape Town with over 1,400 learners had no central digital presence. Manual processes for applications, reporting, and communication caused inefficiencies and delays.

The Solution

We built a comprehensive ASP.NET MVC web application with five integrated portals:

🏛️ Public Website
School information, news, events, online applications, sponsor showcase
👨‍🏫 Teacher Portal
Manage learners, create reports, post assignments, communicate with parents
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Parent Portal
View children's profiles, grades, reports, assignments, submit consent forms
🎒 Learner Portal
View assignments, submit work online, access learning materials
⚙️ Administrator Portal
Manage users, process applications, generate reports, track donations
